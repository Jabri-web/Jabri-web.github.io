🌟 About Me

I am Eng. Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Saleh Al-Jabri, an independent Yemeni researcher and software developer. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) in Saudi Arabia.

My scientific and technical work focuses on three main pillars: Theoretical Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, and Yemeni Heritage Documentation. I founded Heaven Al-Jabri and Sindbad Yemen Gateway to serve as a bridge between the authenticity of the past and the ambition of the future.

🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision

To establish "Heaven Al-Jabri" as a global reference for researchers and enthusiasts of Yemeni heritage and theoretical sciences.

Mission

To transparently document and disseminate Yemeni heritage, produce original scientific research that advances human knowledge, and elevate Yemen's name in academic circles.

📚 Research & Specialization

🧮 Theoretical Mathematics

I am developing the "Unified Sindbad Theory" – an attempt to unify different branches of mathematics. I believe that mathematics is the language of the universe, and understanding it deeply is the key to unlocking its mysteries.

⚛️ Theoretical Physics

I explore the foundations of the universe, spacetime, and unification theories. I strive to understand the laws governing the smallest particles and largest galaxies. The human quest to understand the cosmos is the greatest adventure of the human mind.

🏛️ Yemeni Heritage Documentation

Yemen is the cradle of civilizations. I work to document its landmarks: Old Sana'a, Shibam Hadramawt, Socotra, Marib, Saada, and more. Through photos, stories, and historical research, we preserve this legacy for future generations and showcase Yemen's beauty and grandeur to the world.

🚀 Key Software Projects

I develop open-source projects via GitHub and my personal website:

  • Sindbad Yemen: The main portal for heritage and documentation projects.
  • Yemen Bird & Yemen Ankaboot: Software projects for research and digital archiving.
  • RieOS / RiemannOS: Experimental systems related to my research in the Riemann zeta function and mathematics.
  • Jabri Lab: A digital space for software experiments and research sharing.
  • Unified Sindbad Theory: A symbolic mathematical thesis (Z + C + A = 1).
