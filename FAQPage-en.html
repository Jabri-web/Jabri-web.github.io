Quick answers about the Sindbad Theory and Heaven Al-Jabri research

Who is Eng. Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Al-Jabri?

Eng. Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Al-Jabri is an independent Yemeni researcher and software developer, holding a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM). He is the founder of the Heaven Al-Jabri platform and the Sindbad Yemen Gateway, specializing in Theoretical Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, and documenting Yemeni heritage.

What is the Sindbad Unified Theory?

The Sindbad Unified Theory (Z + C + A = 1) is a mathematical and symbolic thesis aimed at unifying different branches of mathematics into a cohesive framework. It is inspired by Yemen's rich heritage and the adventurous spirit embodied by Sindbad.

What are the most prominent software projects of Eng. Abdulla Al-Jabri?

Sindbad Yemen, Yemen Bird, Yemen Ankboot, RieOS, RiemannOS, Jabri Lab, and the Sindbad Unified Theory.

What are the research fields of Eng. Abdulla Al-Jabri?

Theoretical Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, and Documenting Yemeni Heritage: Old Sana'a, Shibam Hadramawt, Socotra, Marib, and Saada.

What is the Heaven Al-Jabri platform?

A digital platform bridging the authenticity of the past with the ambition of the future, by documenting Yemeni heritage and publishing scientific research in mathematics and theoretical physics.

What is the vision of Heaven Al-Jabri?

To become a global reference for researchers and enthusiasts of Yemeni heritage and theoretical sciences.
