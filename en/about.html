🌟 About Me

I am Eng. Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Al-Jabri, an independent Yemeni researcher and software developer. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) in Saudi Arabia.

My scientific and technical work focuses on three main pillars: Theoretical Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, and Documenting Yemeni Heritage. I founded the Heaven Al-Jabri platform and the Sindbad Yemen Gateway to serve as a bridge between the authenticity of the past and the ambition of the future.

🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision

For "Heaven Al-Jabri" to become a global reference for researchers and enthusiasts of Yemeni heritage and theoretical sciences.

Mission

To document and publish Yemeni heritage with full transparency, and to present authentic scientific research that contributes to the advancement of human knowledge and elevates Yemen's name in scientific forums worldwide.

📚 Research & Specializations

🧮 Theoretical Mathematics

I am working on developing the "Sindbad Unified Theory", an attempt to unify different branches of mathematics into a coherent framework. I firmly believe that mathematics is the language of the universe, and deep understanding is the key to solving the riddles of existence.

⚛️ Theoretical Physics

I investigate the fundamentals of the universe, space-time, and unified theories. I strive to understand the laws governing the smallest particles and the largest galaxies. Humanity's journey to understand the cosmos is the greatest adventure of the human mind.

🏛️ Documenting Yemeni Heritage

Yemen is the cradle of civilizations. I work on documenting its landmarks: Old Sana'a, Shibam Hadramawt, Socotra, Marib, Saada, and others. Through photos, stories, and historical research, we preserve this legacy for future generations and showcase Yemen's beauty and grandeur to the world.

🚀 Key Software Projects

I develop a range of open‑source projects via GitHub and my own website:

  • Sindbad Yemen: The main portal for heritage and documentation projects for Yemen.
  • Yemen Bird & Yemen Ankboot: Software projects for digital archiving and research.
  • RieOS / RiemannOS: Experimental systems related to my research on the Riemann zeta function and mathematics.
  • Jabri Lab: A digital space for running experiments and sharing research.
  • Sindbad Unified Theory: A mathematical framework (Z + C + A = 1).
