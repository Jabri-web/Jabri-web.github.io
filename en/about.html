🌟 Biography

I am Eng. Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Saleh Al-Jabri, an independent Yemeni researcher and software developer. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) in Saudi Arabia.

For years, I have worked in the IT sector, holding engineering positions in major telecommunications companies. However, my true passion lies in three main pillars: Theoretical Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, and Yemeni Heritage Documentation. I founded Heaven Al-Jabri and the Sindbad Yemen Gateway to serve as a bridge connecting the authenticity of the past with the ambition of the future.

🏛️ About Heaven Al-Jabri (The Gateway)

Heaven Al-Jabri is not just a website; it is a comprehensive cultural and scientific project. It was founded to be a global reference for researchers and enthusiasts of Yemeni heritage and theoretical sciences.

⚙️ Infrastructure of the Gateway

The gateway relies on an advanced software architecture to ensure sustainability and speed:

  • GitHub: As the Single Source of Truth for all code.
  • Vercel: For hosting and automatic updates (Continuous Deployment) as a safe and fast alternative in Yemen.
  • Files: The gateway contains over 248 files (including HTML, PDF papers, Python codes, and datasets).
  • Indexing: The site is currently closed under archiving to ensure full documentation in search engines before the official launch.

🏛️ Yemeni Heritage Documentation

Yemen is the cradle of civilizations. I work to document its timeless landmarks and protect them digitally:

  • Old Sana'a: Documenting unique mud-brick architecture.
  • Shibam Hadramawt: The world's oldest skyscrapers.
  • Socotra: Natural beauty and rare biodiversity.
  • Marib & Saada: Documenting ancient history and civilizations.

We archive photos, stories, and historical research to present Yemen to the world in its most beautiful form.
