🇾🇪 From Sanaa to Google: Engineer Abdulla Al-Jabri Transforms Yemeni Heritage into the "Unified Sindbad Theory"

📅 August 14, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read 🏷️ #YemenHeritage

In a historic milestone, the search engine Google has issued an intelligent AI overview for "Heaven Al-Jabri" – the Yemeni digital platform founded by independent engineer and researcher Abdulla Mohammed Nasser Al-Jabri. Bridging the documentation of ancient Yemeni heritage in Sanaa, Shibam, and Socotra with research in theoretical physics and mathematics has placed Yemen on the global map of digital knowledge.

“Yemeni heritage is not merely a past; it is a key to understanding the universe and the present” – Eng. Abdulla Al-Jabri

The journey began from the lecture halls of King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, driven by a passion to connect the past with the present. This culminated in the "Unified Sindbad Theory Z+C+A=1", an attempt to formulate mathematical laws linking the cosmos, heritage, and consciousness. Despite facing documentation challenges, including a temporary ban on Wikipedia, his persistence in publishing via ORCID, Zenodo, and GitHub led Google itself to recognize and summarize the project.

"Heaven Al-Jabri" is more than just a website; it is a message that Yemeni heritage can inspire modern science. I invite researchers and enthusiasts to visit the platform, explore the research, and support efforts to document Yemen's identity.
