🏙️ Historic Cities

🏙️

Old Sana'a

An ancient walled city known for its mud-brick houses decorated with geometric patterns. A UNESCO World Heritage site.

🌊

Aden

Aden is known for its cisterns, golden coasts, and tourist resorts. Yemen's gateway to the Indian Ocean.

🏰

Shibam Hadramawt

The "Manhattan of the Desert" — the world's oldest skyscrapers made of mudbrick. A World Heritage site.

🏝️

Socotra

Island of dragons and natural wonders, home to the rarest plants on Earth. The jewel of the Indian Ocean.

💎 Landmarks & Heritage

🕌

Ancient Mosques

Historic mosques dating back to the early centuries of Islam in Yemen, like the Great Mosque of Sana'a.

🎨

Handicrafts

Yemeni silver, daggers (janbiya), textiles, and pottery. Crafts passed down through generations.

📜

Manuscripts

Historic libraries containing rare manuscripts on science, astronomy, medicine, and jurisprudence.
